Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about orders, networks, pricing and USDT transfers.

Do I need an account?

No customer account is required in the initial version. The platform uses the information supplied during the order to process and identify the purchase.

Why do I pay a network fee?

Blockchain networks require transaction fees. The applicable fee is displayed before the order is submitted.

Will I receive the exact USDT amount?

The USDT amount requested by the customer is separate from the displayed network fee. The order summary shows both values before payment.

Can I use any wallet?

The wallet must support the selected network. The customer is responsible for verifying the address and network before submitting an order.

Can blockchain transfers be cancelled?

Blockchain transfers may be irreversible once broadcast and confirmed. Customers should verify all transaction details before submitting an order.

How is payment confirmed?

In the initial version, payments are reviewed and confirmed manually by the platform operator before the USDT transfer is processed.
