RISK DISCLOSURE

Understand the risks

Digital assets and blockchain transactions are not risk-free.

Blockchain risk

Transactions sent to an incorrect address or through an incompatible network may be irreversible.

Price risk

The market value of digital assets and fiat currencies can change. Platform pricing may also vary between markets.

Network risk

Blockchain networks may experience congestion, delays, outages or increased transaction costs.

Operational risk

Orders may require manual review. Processing times can vary depending on payment verification and network conditions.

Regulatory risk

Digital-asset services may be regulated differently depending on the country or jurisdiction. Customers are responsible for complying with applicable laws and restrictions.
