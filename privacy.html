Privacy Policy

How information supplied through the platform may be used to operate and support orders.

Information collected

Depending on the functionality used, the platform may process information such as a customer name, contact information, wallet address, order details, payment information and technical information.

Why information is used

  • To create and identify orders.
  • To communicate about an order.
  • To provide customer support.
  • To prevent abuse and fraudulent activity.
  • To comply with applicable legal obligations.

Wallet addresses

Blockchain addresses may be publicly visible on their respective networks. Customers should understand that blockchain transactions can be publicly traceable.

Data retention

Information may be retained for operational, security, accounting or legal purposes for periods appropriate to the applicable requirements.

Third parties

Certain technical or payment services may process information where necessary to operate the platform. The final production version should identify those providers specifically.
